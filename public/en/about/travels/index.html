Mostafa Shawky

Mostafa Shawky's journeys abroad

Mostafa Shawky kept his own list of journeys abroad. He saw each one as “a practical study” that added to his architectural store, and made an album of photographs after every trip. Many were tied directly to his projects.

From the list he prepared himself (portfolio book, pp. 281–284). Journeys made only for medical treatment are omitted here. 39 journeys between 1954 and 1984
