Nasser Institute for Research and Treatment

From 1970 to 1978 he was consultant to the Nasser Institute authority. He wrote the detailed brief and produced the architectural designs for a 600-bed general hospital with integrated research laboratories.

In 1970 he visited the leading modern hospitals, research laboratories and medical-equipment plants in and around Paris and made a technical analysis of their main elements.
